# Chrome Web Store Listing

## Extension Name
QuickTodo - Always-on Task Reminder

## Short Description (132文字以内)
A minimal todo list that keeps your top priority task visible at the bottom of every page. No account needed. All data stored locally.

## Detailed Description

**Stay focused on what matters most.**

QuickTodo is a lightweight, distraction-free todo list that keeps your highest-priority task visible at the bottom of every webpage — without opening anything.

### Key Features

- **Always-visible task bar** — Your top priority task appears as a slim bar at the bottom of every page, so you never lose track of what to do next.
- **Quick add** — Press Alt+Shift+T to open the popup, type your task, and hit Enter. Done.
- **Keyboard shortcut** — Press Alt+Shift+D to mark the top task as complete without touching your mouse.
- **Drag to reprioritize** — Drag tasks up or down to reorder them instantly.
- **Click to complete** — Click any task row to toggle completion.
- **100% local** — All data is stored on your device. No account, no server, no tracking.

### How It Works

1. Click the QuickTodo icon (or press Alt+Shift+T) to open the popup.
2. Type a task and press Enter to add it.
3. Your top task immediately appears at the bottom of the current page.
4. Drag tasks to reprioritize. Press Alt+Shift+D to complete the top task.

### Privacy

QuickTodo stores all data locally using chrome.storage.local. Nothing is ever sent to any server. See the full privacy policy for details.
